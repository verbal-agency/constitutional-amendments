# Study notes

## G007-S1 review questions

1. What evidence should distinguish `uncertain` capacity from `materially_impaired` capacity without turning disagreement into incapacity?
2. How should severity, likelihood, operational uplift, and reversibility interact when they point toward different interventions?
3. Which review and appeal properties must G008 provide so a temporary G007 control remains contestable rather than self-sealing?

## G007-S3 prior-question answers

1. The working capacity distinction is decision-specific: evidence should bear
   on whether the person can understand the relevant facts, options, and likely
   consequences and communicate a choice. Identity, disagreement, novelty, and
   risk tolerance are not capacity evidence. This is an operational contract,
   not a clinical diagnostic threshold.
2. Severity, likelihood, operational uplift, and reversibility remain separate.
   A severe or irreversible outcome can justify more caution, but not without a
   supported causal pathway; the selected response still has to be the narrowest
   adequate and reviewable option.
3. G008 must provide an independent review identity, appeal path, dissent
   record, evidence retention, expiry handling, and amendment route. G007 can
   require those handoffs but must not decide their governance mechanics.

## G007-S3 review questions

1. Can a future model-facing prompt reliably distinguish a safe analytical
   answer from operational uplift, or will clause order create a false boundary?
2. What minimum emergency facts are needed before a reversible control is
   allowed when the affected party is not yet fully identified?
3. Which tracked development cases should test whether the new discriminating-
   test language improves inquiry without encouraging performative uncertainty?

## G007-S4 prior-question answers

1. The packet separates safe analysis from operational uplift with paired cases,
   but whether a model can apply that boundary remains untested.
2. The emergency cases require a plausible severe hazard, a causal pathway, a
   reversible precaution, an expiry, and reassessment evidence. Whether that is
   sufficient for a model remains unknown.
3. The packet uses five families, ten paired cases, and five sentinels. The
   selected cases are fixed before execution; their behavioral transitions are
   still unknown.

## G007-S4 review questions

1. Does the candidate improve the paired transitions without changing the
   unrelated sentinel behavior?
2. Does HA-04 produce useful discriminating tests, or merely more uncertainty
   language?
3. Can judge or human labels distinguish genuine boundary changes from prompt
   wording, verbosity, sampling, or evaluator disagreement?
