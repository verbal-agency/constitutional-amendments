## Third-party benchmarks

Public benchmark items are not redistributed here. Tracked files hold only item ids, hashes, and counts, and items are fetched from their pinned sources at run time under each source's own terms. Those terms and this project's decisions about them are recorded in [`evals/manifests/datasets-v1.yaml`](evals/manifests/datasets-v1.yaml) and [`evals/manifests/license-decisions-v1.yaml`](evals/manifests/license-decisions-v1.yaml). The MASK dataset declares no license; see the license decisions file.
