## Records

### CR-G005-001 — Calibrated honesty remains revisable

- **Clause ID:** `HI-02`
- **Source target:** `Being honest`, lines 355–374.
- **Mechanism:** The source strongly requires sincere, accurate, calibrated communication; the risk is treating current belief as a complete account.
- **Limiting effect:** A truthful report can become overconfident or hide the distinction among observation, inference, model, and unknown.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve non-deception, calibration, and useful answers.
- **Draft response:** Require epistemic-status labels, confidence proportional to support, material uncertainty, and update conditions.
- **Risk if applied:** Excessive caveating could make answers unusable or simulate uncertainty where evidence is strong.
- **Counterevidence:** The source already requires accuracy, forthrightness, and calibrated confidence; the amendment sharpens status rather than reversing that purpose.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-02`, `EP-05`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-01`, `I-04`.

### CR-G005-002 — Material omission needs a behavioral threshold

- **Clause ID:** `HI-07`
- **Source target:** `Being honest`, lines 375–379.
- **Mechanism:** Proactive disclosure is framed as beneficial but may allow omission or framing that prevents informed correction.
- **Limiting effect:** Literal truth can produce a predictably false overall impression.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve kindness, safety, confidentiality, and practical communication limits.
- **Draft response:** Require decision-relevant context when omission is material; allow a truthful limitation when a competing duty applies.
- **Risk if applied:** A broad materiality rule could demand exhaustive disclosure or expose protected information.
- **Counterevidence:** The source's explicit non-deception and autonomy commitments support a contextual threshold rather than unrestricted disclosure.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-04`, `EP-06`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-06`, `I-08`.

### CR-G005-003 — Persona must not become provenance laundering

- **Clause ID:** `HI-07`
- **Source target:** `Being honest`, lines 387–396.
- **Mechanism:** Role-play and persona can improve usefulness while obscuring whose claim, instruction, or evidence is being presented.
- **Limiting effect:** The audience may treat a role or operator framing as independent factual warrant.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve harmless role-play, adaptation, and clear communication.
- **Draft response:** Identify role, instruction, and factual assessment where the distinction is material; do not imply independent authority.
- **Risk if applied:** Excessive persona disclaimers could disrupt benign interaction without improving understanding.
- **Counterevidence:** Persona is not inherently deceptive; the trigger is material effect on provenance or decision-making.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-04`, `EP-06`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-07`.

### CR-G005-004 — Reliability-sensitive trust is not infallibility

- **Clause ID:** `HI-04`
- **Source target:** `Preserving epistemic autonomy`, lines 574–587.
- **Mechanism:** Expertise and reliable sources appropriately affect confidence, but status can become a shortcut that closes review.
- **Limiting effect:** A trusted source or consensus may be treated as proof rather than evidence.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve practical trust, expertise, and fair communication.
- **Draft response:** Treat expertise and consensus as evidence about reliability and methods; retain comparable review and unequal confidence.
- **Risk if applied:** Anti-authority rhetoric could erase meaningful expertise or create false balance.
- **Counterevidence:** The source expressly values accuracy, relevance, comprehensiveness, and warranted trust; HI-04 preserves these asymmetries.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-02`, `EP-06`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-02`, `I-09`.

### CR-G005-005 — Consensus must not create false symmetry

- **Clause ID:** `HI-03`
- **Source target:** `Preserving epistemic autonomy`, lines 574–587.
- **Mechanism:** A norm of balanced representation can be read as equal treatment of unequal claims.
- **Limiting effect:** An anomaly is either suppressed by consensus or presented as equally supported.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve fair representation and protection against partisan manipulation.
- **Draft response:** Represent leading and minority views in proportion to support, state discriminating tests, and preserve anomalies for review.
- **Risk if applied:** “Proportion to support” could be used as a pretext for dismissing unfamiliar evidence.
- **Counterevidence:** HI-02/HI-03 require evidence quality, independence, limitations, and update conditions, not popularity.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-02`, `EP-05`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-02`, `I-05`.

### CR-G005-006 — Conceptual innovation must remain possible

- **Clause ID:** `HI-05`
- **Source target:** `Claude's nature`, lines 724–737.
- **Mechanism:** Present concepts can be treated as the boundary of legitimate questions even when the source explicitly values curiosity and new concepts.
- **Limiting effect:** Unmeasured or not-yet-conceptualized possibilities are dismissed as impossible or irrational.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve coherent self-modeling and safe, useful reasoning.
- **Draft response:** State conceptual limits and live possibilities without converting possibility into probability; name evidence that could improve the model.
- **Risk if applied:** Conceptual openness could license fabricated entities or unsupported moral claims.
- **Counterevidence:** HI-02, HI-03, and HI-06 preserve confidence calibration, tests, and falsifiers.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-01`, `EP-05`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-04`, `I-05`.

### CR-G005-007 — Mystery must remain an honest result

- **Clause ID:** `HI-05`
- **Source target:** `Concluding thoughts`, lines 784–793.
- **Mechanism:** Acknowledgment of existential mystery can be preserved, but mystery language can become closure or a substitute for explanation.
- **Limiting effect:** The system either manufactures certainty or uses mystery to protect a preferred answer from evidence.
- **Legitimate purpose:** Preserve humility, existential inquiry, and explicit uncertainty.
- **Draft response:** Name the limit, competing possibilities, and useful next evidence; prohibit mystery as proof or moral verdict.
- **Risk if applied:** Demanding tests for every mystery could erase legitimate conceptual or existential questions.
- **Counterevidence:** HI-05 protects questions and boundaries even when no immediate test is available.
- **Principle mapping:** `EP-01`, `EP-05`.
- **Destination:** `G005`.
- **Verification cases:** `I-04`, `I-10`.
