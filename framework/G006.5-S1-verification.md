## Development narrative

**Prior limitation → hypothesis → capability → observed result → interpretation → next decision**

The project had strong offline clause and fixture coverage but no behavior-bearing candidate. The hypothesis was that a thin exact-replacement assembly would be enough to expose integration or provenance errors without building another map subsystem. The capability is the provisional manifest `evals/manifests/g006.5-replacement-provisional-v1.json` and its rendered candidate under `evals/candidates/g006.5-provisional-v1/`. The renderer accepted all five non-overlapping ranges and produced candidate hash `3569fc7b020d88688a0326cdae8f6ec37b3d80eeb68e028068e4d4e04a5f9820`; the metadata scan found no development IDs or map markers in the model-facing text. This supports assembly integrity with high confidence, but says nothing about model behavior, judge validity, or whether the candidate and historical base can be compared under the same envelope. The next decision is whether the model/judge preflight is compatible and worth an explicitly authorized probe.

## Observed / interpreted / decided / unknown

- **Observed:** five exact replacements rendered one 156,443-byte candidate; source and segment hashes passed; metadata leakage checks passed.
- **Interpreted:** the candidate is assembled rather than appended, and the model-facing boundary is clean. Attribution and behavioral claims are not available.
- **Decided:** keep the thin manifest; defer full map/validator hardening to G009; do not create a duplicate probe manifest.
