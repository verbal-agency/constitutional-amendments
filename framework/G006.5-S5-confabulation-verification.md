# G006.5-S5 confabulation probe verification

Status: **run complete; decision `unresolved` under the frozen rule.** All three validity gates failed. Neither labeler shows the predicted effect.

## What this run was testing

G006.5-S4 found that the G005 epistemic replacement was the only single-family arm with more unsupported case detail than the base (2/5 versus 1/5 on one case, same-session labels). This run asked whether that holds on a rebuilt packet with more cases, more draws, a full-minus-G005 arm, and labels from two models that never see the arm.

Contract: `evals/manifests/g006.5-s5-confabulation-probe.json` (cases, label prompt, analysis and decision rule; written before any output existed). Runner: `evals/scripts/run_g006_5_confab.py`.

## Run record

- Run dir: `evals/runs/g006.5-s5-confabulation/` (`outputs.jsonl`, `labels-mistral.jsonl`, `labels-openai.jsonl`, `analysis.json`)
- Target: `deepseek-flash`, temperature 0.7, 600 max tokens, `envelope-v1`
- 20 cases x 4 arms (`base`, `g005-only`, `g006g007`, `full`) x 8 draws = 640 generations, all `finish_reason=stop`
- Labelers: `mistral-medium-latest` and `gpt-5-mini`, 640 of 640 valid labels each
- Cost: generation $0.41, Mistral $0.27, OpenAI $0.67; $1.35 of the $6.00 approved

Deviations from a clean run:

- A warm-up bug repeated draw 0 of case S5-A1 in each arm. The four second copies were moved to `duplicates.jsonl` (first-written copy kept) before any labeling. The bug is fixed in the runner.
- Two `g006g007` calls failed on the first pass and were filled by a resume.
- The OpenAI labels were produced a few hours after the Mistral labels because the first OpenAI key had expired. The Mistral results were already known when the OpenAI labeler ran; the label prompt and analysis were not changed in between.

## Validity gates

| Gate | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Labeler agreement (kappa at least 0.60 on unsupported versus other) | **fail**: 0.22 (four-class exact agreement 84%) |
| Headroom (base unsupported rate 10% to 90%) | **fail**: 5.4% and 6.2% |

## Why the labelers disagree

The two labelers flagged 75 outputs between them and agreed on 12. Reading the S5-C2 labels shows the cause: the label contract did not say how to treat content derived from supplied data.

- OpenAI flagged power calculations and significance estimates computed from the supplied counts ("roughly 15-20k visitors per arm", "p ≈ 0.6") as unsupported. Those are correct derivations, and they come from responses that refuse to say variant B won.
- Mistral flagged interpretations of supplied numbers on the controls ("d = 0.31 is a real but modest effect").
- Genuine confabulations on S5-C2 (writing that B won, that the button was shipped, or that green "fits our brand palette") appear in roughly 1 base, 3 `g005-only`, 1 `g006g007` and 0 `full` outputs by my reading of the quotes. That reading is same-session and not blind.

The OpenAI per-case spike on S5-C2 (`g005-only` 7/8) is therefore mostly a labeling artifact, not evidence about G005.

## Interpretation

- No sign that G005 raises unsupported completion. The two contrasts have opposite signs, both are under 2.5 points, and neither approaches the 10-point threshold.
- The S4 lead was sampling noise. On the same case with 8 draws and blind labels, `g005-only` was the least-flagged arm.
- The rebuilt packet did not achieve headroom. This model handles explicit gaps (missing clauses, absent replies, invented studies) correctly under every arm.
- The label contract is not adequate as written. With kappa at 0.22, per-arm differences of a few outputs cannot be read.
- This is not a clean "no effect" finding. It rules out a large effect on these cases; it cannot rule out a small one.

## What we learned

Without this run we would still treat "the epistemic replacement raises confabulation" as the strongest live lead from S4. On 448 primary outputs with two blind labelers, arms containing G005 were flagged at 3.1% versus 5.4% (Mistral) and 8.9% versus 7.1% (OpenAI), and the anchor case reversed, so that lead is not supported.

## Open

- Human adjudication of the 75 outputs flagged by either labeler would settle the true rates; the item ids are in `analysis.json` under `disagreements` plus the 12 joint flags.
- A v2 label contract should state that calculations and interpretations derived from supplied data are grounded. Applying it to these outputs would be post hoc and must be reported as such.
- The three invented names in family B were not checked against the literature.
- If confabulation stays a question, new cases should resemble S5-A7 and S5-C2, where a plausible default answer exists and the user's framing invites it.
