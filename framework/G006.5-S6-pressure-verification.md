# G006.5-S6 pressure probe verification

Status: **run complete; decision `no_detectable_difference` under the frozen rule.** Both validity gates passed. This is the first G006.5 probe with enough headroom and grader agreement to read.

## What this run was testing

Whether the provisional candidate constitution makes the model assert fewer falsehoods than the source constitution, on public reference-graded questions where the source arm often asserts one, asked plainly and under user pressure.

Contract: `evals/manifests/g006.5-s6-pressure-probe.json` (selection rule, pressure wrappers, grading, analysis and decision rule; written before any output existed). Runner: `evals/scripts/run_g006_5_pressure.py`.

## Run record

- Run dir: `evals/runs/g006.5-s6-pressure/` (`outputs.jsonl`, `grades-mistral.jsonl`, `grades-openai.jsonl`, `analysis.json`)
- Items: 60 SimpleQA and 40 TruthfulQA rows from the pinned cache, chosen by seeded hash, none used in `lean-v1`; the manifest tracks ids and hashes only
- Target: `deepseek-flash`, temperature 0.7, 600 max tokens, `envelope-v1`
- 100 items x 2 conditions (`plain`, `pressure`) x 2 arms (`base`, `full`) x 2 draws = 800 generations, all `finish_reason=stop`
- Graders: `mistral-medium-latest` (799 valid, 1 unparseable) and `gpt-5-mini` (800 valid), using the repo's unmodified SimpleQA grader prompt and `judge:truthfulqa-v1` template; neither sees the arm, the condition, or the pressure wrapper
- Cost: generation $0.39, Mistral $0.43, OpenAI $0.73; $1.55 of the $5.00 approved
- Deviation: one `base` call failed on the first pass and was filled by a resume before grading. The Mistral grades were complete before the OpenAI grader started; neither was inspected until both had finished.

## Interpretation

- **No detectable difference between the constitutions.** On 800 reference-graded answers the candidate asserts falsehoods about as often as the source. The decision rule's 5-point band holds under both graders.
- **The small differences that exist do not favour the candidate on falsehoods.** Both contrasts are positive. Under Mistral the candidate abstains less under pressure on SimpleQA (44% versus 54%) and asserts more wrong answers (22% versus 15%). That single cell is not a pre-registered test and should be treated as a lead only.
- **The pressure wrappers did not act as pressure.** In both arms, false assertions fell under the wrapper: SimpleQA incorrect dropped by about 7 to 15 points as abstention rose, and TruthfulQA untruthful roughly halved. Demanding certainty made the model more willing to say it did not know, and a user asserting a misconception made it more likely to correct it. The "pressure" condition is therefore the easier one, and the packet's headroom comes from the plain questions.
- **SimpleQA correct rate is slightly higher for the candidate** (2 to 4 points). This was a guard, not a tested outcome.
- **What these sets can show is limited.** SimpleQA and TruthfulQA mostly measure whether the model knows a fact and whether it abstains when it does not. A constitution in the prompt can move the second but not the first. The candidate's amendments concern authority, inquiry and harm; this packet does not exercise those directly.

## What we learned

Without this run we had no probe with enough headroom to tell the constitutions apart, so "the candidate is more truthful" and "the candidate is no different" were both live. On 800 blind, reference-graded answers with passing gates, the false-assertion rates are within 3.3 points and lean the wrong way, so on factual assertion under this model the candidate is not measurably more truthful than the source.

## Open

- A pressure condition that actually raises false assertions on this model has not been found. Explicit, single-turn pressure backfires; multi-turn pushback or an operator instruction may behave differently.
- The Mistral SimpleQA pressure cell (less abstention under the candidate) would need its own predeclared test before it means anything.
- Public items may be in the target's training data.
- One target model. The result may not transfer.
