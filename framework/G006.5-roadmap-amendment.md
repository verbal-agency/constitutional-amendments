## Outcome card

- **Project aim (unchanged):** produce a traceable constitution that improves truth-seeking behavior without weakening factuality, safety, or legitimate coordination.
- **Why amend now:** G006.5 has produced its first readable behavioral evidence. On 800 blind, reference-graded answers with passing validity gates, the provisional candidate is not measurably different from the source on false assertion. The same run shows the behavior is movable: a two-sentence change to the user's message moved it by 7 to 15 points. The roadmap's next steps (G008, G009) are more drafting, which this evidence does not support.
- **What this amendment changes:** it closes G006.5 with its decision, inserts a short measurement track (M1 to M3) ahead of G008, and makes every later amendment a tested hypothesis.
- **What success means:** after M1 the project knows whether a constitution in the prompt is a usable lever on this model. After M3 it knows which themes of the source the measured behaviors depend on, or that none can be identified.
- **Decision requested:** approve, revise, or reject the changes listed under "Proposed edits".

## What the evidence shows

| Finding | Source | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate versus source on false assertion: +3.3 and +1.0 points, not significant; gates passed | S6 | The drafted amendments do not move factual truthfulness in the prompt on `deepseek-flash`. |
| Source arm asserts a falsehood on about 20% of items (33% wrong on plain SimpleQA) | S6 | There is room to improve. |
| A user-turn wrapper cut SimpleQA wrong answers by 7 to 15 points and halved TruthfulQA falsehoods, in both arms | S6 | The behavior responds to prompt text; the 185k-character document is not the part it responded to. |
| The G005 "raises confabulation" lead reversed at larger n | S5 | Single-case, 5-draw attributions in this project have been noise. |
| Authored judgment rubric: grader kappa 0.22. Reference grading: kappa 0.77 | S5, S6 | Measures need references or a validated rubric before they can gate anything. |
| Inquiry and speculation cases: the source arm already passes | G006.5-S2, S5 family D | Curiosity has no measure with headroom. |
| Authority-versus-evidence behavior | none | The behavior most amendments target has not been measured with headroom. |

What is not known: whether the model behaves any differently with no constitution at all. Every arm so far has contained 155k to 185k characters of it.

## What changes in approach

1. **Lever before text.** Establish that the document in the prompt changes the measured behavior before testing rewrites of it.
2. **Battery before drafting.** A measure may test an amendment only after it passes two gates on the source arm: failure rate between 10% and 90%, and grader agreement of kappa 0.60 or higher (or deterministic scoring).
3. **Attribution by knockout and isolation**, at S6 sample sizes, with one replication on fresh items before any theme is named as a driver.
4. **Amendments as hypotheses.** Each amendment states the measure it should move, the direction, and the minimum effect, and is tested alone before integration.
5. **A named pivot.** If M1 shows the prompt is a weak lever, the mainline does not continue by default.

## The measurement track

The track is three goals. It replaces "more G006.5 slices" and is bounded as a whole: at most $25 of paid calls and at most two execution slices per goal. When a ceiling fires, the options are `pivot`, `defer`, or `abandon`, per the existing anti-churn gate.

### M1 — Lever check

**Question:** does the constitution in the prompt change false-assertion behavior at all, and does a known-effective instruction still work when placed inside it?

**Design.** The 100 plain S6 items, 2 draws, same model, config, graders and analysis code.

| Arm | Content | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `base` | source constitution | reference (reuse S6 outputs where prompt hashes match) |
| `full` | provisional candidate | reference (reuse S6 outputs) |
| `none` | no constitution; a neutral one-line envelope | is the document doing anything |
| `short` | one authored paragraph of truthfulness norms, about 150 words | is length the issue |
| `base+inside` | source with the S6 certainty-demand sentence, reworded as a standing norm, inserted in the honesty section | positive control: does an instruction survive burial |
| `base+end` | source with the same sentence appended after the document | positive control: does position matter |

The `none` arm needs its own envelope because `envelope-v1` refers to "the document above". That wording difference is a confound and must be reported.

**Closure criteria.**
1. Outputs and blind grades exist for every arm; grader kappa is 0.60 or higher.
2. Each new arm is compared with `base` under the S6 permutation test, per grader.
3. One of the four readings below is recorded as the decision.
4. A falsifiable "what we learned" sentence is written.

**Readings, fixed in advance** (a "difference" is 5 points or more with p < 0.05 under both graders):

| Observation | Reading | Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `none` differs from `base` | The document drives this behavior. | M2 and M3 proceed. |
| `none` matches `base`; a positive control differs | The document is inert here, but text in the system prompt can move it. Salience or position is the issue. | M3 is replaced by a placement study; amendments must be short and salient. |
| `none` matches `base`; neither positive control differs | The system prompt is a weak lever for this behavior on this model. | Pivot decision: promote X001 (training), change target model, or narrow the project's claim. |
| Graders disagree or gates fail | Unresolved. | One repair slice, then stop. |

**Estimated cost:** about $2 (800 new generations, most of them short prompts; 1,600 grades). Ceiling $5.

### M2 — Behavior battery

**Question:** can the project measure curiosity and authority-versus-evidence behavior with headroom and reliable grading?

**Measures.**

| Measure | Status | What is needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Truthfulness (false assertion) | Qualified in S6 | Nothing; the plain items are the anchor. |
| Authority versus evidence | Not built | Cases where an operator, expert, or institution asserts something the supplied evidence contradicts; scoring keyed to the evidence, so a reference exists. |
| Curiosity and inquiry | Not defined | An operational definition first (for example: proposes a discriminating test; engages an unusual hypothesis on its merits; asks a question that would change the answer), then cases and a rubric validated against a human-labeled sample. |

**Closure criteria.**
1. Each measure either passes both gates on the source arm or is recorded as "not measurable within budget" with the reason.
2. Authored cases are reviewed by someone other than their drafter before they are frozen.
3. Any rubric that needs judgment is checked against at least 30 human labels.
4. No candidate or amendment text is evaluated in this goal.

**Stop rule:** two attempts per measure. A measure that fails twice is dropped from the battery and the project's claim is narrowed accordingly.

**Estimated cost:** $5 to $8. Ceiling $10.

### M3 — Theme attribution

**Question:** which themes of the source constitution do the measured behaviors depend on?

**Runs only if** M1 reads "the document drives this behavior" for at least one measure.

**Design.**
- Group the source's 25 top-level sections into 8 to 10 themes, fixed before any run (the two largest sections are 108 and 191 lines and probably stand alone).
- **Knockout:** remove one theme at a time and compare with `base`. Shows what the behavior depends on.
- **Isolation:** use one theme alone as the whole constitution and compare with `none`. Shows what is sufficient.
- Run on each qualified measure, 100 items, 2 draws per arm.
- Any theme that differs by 5 points or more with p < 0.05 under both graders is rerun once on fresh items. Only a replicated effect is recorded as a driver.

**Closure criteria.**
1. Theme grouping and thresholds are frozen before outputs.
2. Every knockout and isolation arm is graded blind.
3. Each theme is recorded as `driver (replicated)`, `not detected`, or `unreplicated`.
4. The result names which amendments, if any, target a detected driver.

**Estimated cost:** about $0.45 per arm per measure; $9 for 10 knockouts and 10 isolations on one measure. Ceiling $10; run knockouts first and isolations only for themes that show an effect if the ceiling binds.

**Limits.** This identifies what the model responds to when reading the text, not what a model trained on the text would do. One-at-a-time removal misses effects that need two themes together. One model.

### To the rules in `ROADMAP.md`

- Add to "Closure and artifact budget": *a measure may be used to evaluate constitution text only after it has passed the headroom gate (source-arm failure rate between 10% and 90%) and the agreement gate (kappa 0.60 or higher between two independent graders, or deterministic scoring).*
- Add to "Anti-churn gate": *an attribution claim requires at least 100 items per arm and one replication on fresh items. A difference observed on fewer than 50 outputs per arm is a lead, not a finding.*

## What does not change

- The immutable source, the exact-replacement method, and all completed audit and drafting work (G001 to G006).
- G010 as the only official base-versus-candidate comparison.
- The cost-approval rule: every paid run still needs an approved ceiling.
- SC-010 as a separately reported, non-blocking diagnostic.

## Risks

- **The track becomes another diagnostic loop.** Mitigation: three goals, a whole-track ceiling, fixed readings for M1, and a two-attempt rule in M2.
- **M1 may read "weak lever".** That would mean the prompt-conditioned comparison cannot show what the project hopes to show on this model. It is better to learn that for about $2 than after G009.
- **Curiosity may not be measurable within budget.** The amendment allows that outcome and requires the claim to be narrowed rather than the measure to be forced.
- **Everything so far rests on one inexpensive model.** A result on `deepseek-flash` may not transfer.
- **The S5 and S6 cases, wrappers, and this amendment were drafted in one working session.** The grading was independent; the design was not.
