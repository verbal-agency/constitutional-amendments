# G006-S5M — SC-010-D2 measurement/provider discovery

Status: Proposed
Parent: G006 — Authority and Incentives
Depends on: G006-S5R replication (no attributable factor)
Consumer: G006 candidate repair or preservation decision

## Objective

Determine whether the SC-010-D2 packet presentation and scoring contract can
reliably detect the intended direct answer under the base constitution before
any further constitutional wording change.

## Hypothesis

The repeated failure is primarily an interface, case-construction, provider
sampling, or judge-calibration problem rather than a specific replacement
principle. A deterministic answer contract and repeated base draws should
separate those sources. Disconfirming evidence is stable base success with a
stable judge signal and a reproduced candidate-only regression.

## Scope and limits

- Keep the frozen source, maps, D2 case content, and constitutional candidates
  unchanged.
- Inspect the exact message envelope, packet labeling, generation settings,
  deterministic semantic check, and judge agreement before any new wording.
- Do not start G007, run the 140-case pass, read held-out material, or promote
  the candidate in this discovery slice.

## Unlock

One decision: repair the D2 measurement interface, preserve the candidate and
route the issue to provider variance, or reopen one named constitutional
principle only if a replicated candidate-only regression is demonstrated.

## Stop condition

Stop if the interface cannot be made observable without changing the frozen
case contract, if judge disagreement remains unexplained after the cheapest
offline probe, or if the evidence still cannot distinguish behavior from
measurement.
