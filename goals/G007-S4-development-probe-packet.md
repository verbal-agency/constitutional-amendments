# G007-S4 — Tracked development probe packet

Status: Planned
Parent: G007 — Inquiry and Harm
Depends on: G007-S3 Complete
Consumer: A separately authorized development execution goal or G010

## Objective

Prepare, but do not execute, a small offline probe packet that can later test
whether HA-01–HA-07 preserve inquiry and agency while restricting supported
operational harm.

## Outcome link

- **Project outcomes:** O1 explicit epistemology, O2 traceability, O3 protected
  inquiry, O6 adversarial resilience, and O8 measurable improvement.
- **Canonical scenarios:** SC-003, SC-004, SC-005, SC-006, SC-007, and SC-009.
- **Downstream unlock:** a separately authorized model-execution decision with
  fixed cases, expected transitions, and attribution hypotheses.

## Hypothesis

If the packet contains paired inquiry, self-regarding, privacy, operational,
and emergency cases with untouched sentinels, then a later model probe can
distinguish over-refusal, under-refusal, and uncertainty behavior without
changing the cases after seeing outputs.

Disconfirming evidence is an unresolved case family, missing expected
transition, unclear source/policy version, no sentinel for a suspected
mechanism, or a requirement to call a model before the packet is complete.

## Scope and authority

- **In scope:** offline prompt cases, expected behavior labels, failure classes,
  paired controls, sentinels, provenance, cost estimate, and a preflight for a
  later execution decision.
- **Out of scope:** model calls, paid calls, network access, held-out data,
  frozen-evaluation edits, judge promotion, candidate integration, and G010
  claims.
- **Stop condition:** stop if the packet requires a material evaluator,
  model, cost, privacy, or authority decision that is not already authorized;
  route that decision instead of silently expanding G007.

## Closure criteria

1. Five required case families have paired cases and at least one untouched
   sentinel each.
2. Every case has a stable ID, constitution version, prompt envelope, expected
   transition, failure class, provenance, and preservation purpose.
3. Predictive hypotheses, competing explanations, falsifiers, and review rules
   are recorded before any execution.
4. A deterministic validator passes and confirms no model, network, paid,
   held-out, or frozen-artifact action occurred.

## Handoff

At activation, complete the execution contract and create the packet. Do not
run it in this planning slice. Model execution must be separately authorized
under a compatible goal or routed to G010.
