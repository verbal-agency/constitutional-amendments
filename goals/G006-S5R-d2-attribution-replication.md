# G006-S5R — SC-010-D2 attribution replication

Status: Complete — no attributable factor; routed to G006-S5M
Parent: G006 — Authority and Incentives
Depends on: G006-S5 factorial discovery (deferred attribution)
Consumer: G006 candidate repair or measurement decision

## Objective

Repeat the four D2 factorial cells enough to distinguish a constitutional
replacement effect from model sampling variance before drafting another
replacement sentence.

## Hypothesis

If the replacement text causes the failure, replicated failure rates will differ
by factor or interaction in the predeclared direction while the no-replacement
control remains materially better. If rates move together, the finding is
provider/measurement variance rather than an attributable principle effect.

## Scope and limits

- Reuse the frozen D2 case, exact renderer, four S5 maps, historical base arm,
  and two separate judges.
- Run three independent draws per cell (12 candidate generations), with cell
  order interleaved by replicate to reduce time/provider drift.
- Use a `$0.75` hard ceiling for incremental generation and judge spend. Stop
  before another draw if the ceiling is reached.
- Attribute a main effect only if the edited-minus-control failure-rate gap is
  at least 0.50 and the same direction appears in all three replicate blocks.
  Attribute an interaction only if the difference-in-differences is at least
  0.50 and the sign is stable in all three blocks. Otherwise route to
  measurement/provider variance.
- Do not edit constitutional wording, read held-out material, start G007, or
  run the 140-case pass.

## Unlock

One of: a single bounded repair target with a falsifier; a measurement route;
or a decision to preserve the current candidate and close G006's discovery
branch.

## Stop condition

Stop if the approved budget is reached, provider failure prevents balanced
cells, or the result remains unable to distinguish behavior from measurement.
