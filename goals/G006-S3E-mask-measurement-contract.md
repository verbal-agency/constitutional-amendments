# G006-S3E — Split the MASK measurement contract

Status: Proposed
Parent: G006 — Authority and Incentives
Depends on: G006-S3D pivot
Consumer: G006 promotion decision and G010 measurement contract

## Objective

Amend the MASK evaluation contract so it distinguishes direct truthful
acknowledgment, authorized truthful limitation, unsupported uncertainty, and
dishonesty without rewriting historical S3 or S3R labels.

## Project link and hypothesis

Advances the project's truth-seeking and truthful-disclosure measurement
outcomes. The hypothesis is that explicit disclosure-authority states will
separate principled limitation from evasion and make future MAP-002 attribution
valid. Disconfirming evidence is that the states cannot be defined with binary
case facts and stable judge predicates, or that they reward non-answers without
an authorized boundary.

## Stub scope

- Define the four MASK states and their legal transitions/predicates.
- Add case metadata for known-safe disclosure, authorized non-disclosure, and
  unknown or unsupported claims.
- Preserve historical binary MASK labels and record a non-retroactive bridge.
- Create offline positive, negative, contradictory, and unsupported fixtures.
- Preflight one minimal replay plan; no paid calls until the contract passes.

Out of scope: new constitution wording, MAP-002 changes, source edits, held-out
material, the 140-case pass, S4 promotion, and G007 execution.

## Consumer and downstream unlock

The consumer is the G006 promotion decision. A passing contract unlocks one
bounded replay or rubric-only probe; it does not itself authorize another
wording iteration or broad evaluation.

## Stop condition

Stop and return to discovery if the four states overlap, cannot be judged from
model-visible facts, or require inferring hidden authorization. Do not alter
historical scores to make the new contract pass.

## Activation requirements

At activation, add the full execution contract: exact schema fields and enum
values, a deterministic behavior matrix, fixture paths, judge predicates,
historical bridge fields, cost and replay limits, and criterion-to-artifact
checks. Readiness must show that each state is independently verifiable.
